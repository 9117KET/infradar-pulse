### How did you come up with this idea? Tell us the story.

In my final year at Constructor University, I interned as a full-stack developer at ABiQ, a Dubai-based infrastructure business intelligence company - a direct competitor to MEED and GlobalData - that sells intelligence reports on construction and infrastructure projects across MENA, India, and Africa.

The company had been built on WordPress in 2020 and had just hired a new non-technical CEO. They had two developers, a team of human researchers, and admins who approved the research output. My job: redesign the backend to be AI-native and onboard the CEO on the technical architecture. I essentially became the bridge between the codebase and the business.

Over those months I watched the entire research workflow up close. A typical project brief took: human researchers manually prompting LLMs for deep research, then doing manual website crawling to find stakeholder contacts, then routing through multi-stage approval and rejection queues before anything reached a client. This took days per project. And they were selling the resulting reports for $2,500-$4,000 per region.

The developers wanted to integrate AI properly. But every real AI integration they scoped out would either require cutting the researcher headcount (destroying morale and institutional knowledge) or replacing the report format clients paid for (destroying the revenue model). They ended up patching AI onto the margins - an LLM here, an autocomplete there - without touching the core workflow.

I left that internship with a clear mental model: the workflow itself needs to be rebuilt from scratch, not patched. Each stage of the research process - scraping, contact verification, policy checking, evidence scoring, project comparison - maps exactly to an AI agent. A single human researcher then just confirms the output rather than generating it.

I graduated in December 2025. The next week I started building InfraRadar full-time. Five months in, we have 1,671 verified projects across 140 countries live in production.
